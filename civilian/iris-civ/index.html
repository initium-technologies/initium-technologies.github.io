IRIS CIV

Interior Reconnaissance and Inference System

The first response product that operationally improves police response.

60Seconds

From the 911 call to a confirmed,tracked assailant on everyresponding officer’s screen.

Concept of operations

  1. 01

    911 is called

    A call lands with the emergency communications center. If the building is IRIS-enrolled, then the system starts up.

  2. 02

    Officers receive the picture

    The call is matched to the building, every dispatched officer receives an IRIS link in the call-out package, on their phone or MDT.

  3. 03

    A human verifies the threat

    As units head inbound, Initium’s team checks the building’s live cameras and confirms the suspected threat.

  4. 04

    The live picture is available

    See what IRIS puts on an officer’s phone the second they breach a building.

Tap a point on the screen.

Description

IRIS Civ turns a facility’s existing security cameras into a single live floor plan that shows where every person in the building is, in real time. It connects to the cameras a facility already has, detects every person in view, follows each individual as they move from one camera to the next, and places them on a room-by-room plan of the building.

This live picture is pushed to the responding law enforcement agency the moment an incident is detected, the officers who arrive have all the information before they enter.
